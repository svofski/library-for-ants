GRIPOR
======

Here you can find design files and printables for GRIPOR-pliers and GRIPOR-gears. 
![sketch](/gripor/photo/sketch.jpg)

They can pinch and hold firmly small objects like microSD cards, or wield a small knife.

Pliers
------

This is the model that looks like a common sprue gripper.

![pliers-model](/gripor/photo/model.jpg)
![pliers-diagram](/gripor/photo/pliers1.gif)
![pliers-3d](/gripor/photo/pliers2s.gif)

Extra hardware needed:

 * 4x bearings 3x6x2.5 (ID-OD-thickness)
 * 4x 3mm dia round pins approx 20mm long
 * 2xM2 screws for servo mount (optionally use M2 threaded inserts)
 * M3 threaded inserts optionally recommended for mounting holes
 * 1x MG90S micro servo

Some assembly required

Glue thin pieces of rubber eraser to the inner surfaces of the pliers' tips, it immensely improves the grip.

Gears
-----
This is a parallel gripper. It requires no extra hardware except for the screws. 
It's a good gripper that's easy to build, but it may be a bit less rigid than the pliers version.
